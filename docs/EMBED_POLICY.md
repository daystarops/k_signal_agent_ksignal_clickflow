# K-Signal embed policy

- Article meaning must remain understandable without a third-party player.
- Every YouTube, X, or external embed needs a readable fallback link, platform label, known media credit, accessible title, lazy loading where appropriate, and a safe referrer policy.
- Embeds must not autoplay. Optional player permissions must be minimized.
- External receipt and fallback links open a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- Homepage previews use local still images rather than live embeds. Social exports contain no live embeds.
- Accessibility failures inside a YouTube-controlled cross-origin player are documented as third-party controlled; K-Signal remains responsible for its iframe title, surrounding copy, and fallback.
