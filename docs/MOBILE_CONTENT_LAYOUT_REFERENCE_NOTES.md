# Mobile Content Layout Reference Notes

## Reference pass

Public mobile surfaces were inspected without login, paywall bypass, bulk scraping, personal-data collection, or asset copying. Access limits are part of the finding: article variants, comments, and search can differ by account, publisher, region, or bot protection.

## Lessons

- NYT: strong headline/dek/byline roles, restrained measure, clear section breaks, and recirculation after the reading boundary. Avoid paywall/account patterns and newspaper ornament.
- BuzzFeed: immediate format cues, frequent visual resets, concise blocks, and obvious reaction/navigation affordances. Avoid commerce collisions and engagement bait.
- Vice: tension-led framing, confident media scale, compact metadata, and clear downstream recommendations. Avoid interruption and sensational excess.
- FMKorea: compact board ancestry, time/activity cues, direct comments, and dense but consistent rows. Avoid ad density and treating popularity as truth.
- Naver News: publisher identity, search/category priority, correction/accountability routes, and mobile-specific information reduction. Avoid portal sprawl and opaque blended rankings.
- TheQoo: board/HOT context, terse metadata, direct comment movement, and exceptionally compact lists. Avoid context-light walls and anonymous volume as consensus.

## K-Signal application

Use a 680–720px reading measure, 16–18px body text, 1.6–1.7 line height, one conceptual job per section, and a visual/context reset every two or three sections. Put platform provenance beside the interpretation, keep receipts tappable, then move readers through correction/comments into exactly two Related signals and the separate issue strip. Avoid sticky clutter, unreadable full-page receipts, fake play controls, automatic embeds, and any popularity-as-credibility cue.
